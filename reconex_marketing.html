Clean and reconcile bank statements in minutes.

The Problem

Bank reconciliation is slow, messy, and error-prone.

Many small businesses and bookkeepers spend hours each month wrestling with messy bank statements, manually categorising hundreds of transactions, and trying to reconcile closing balances — often in spreadsheets that break, duplicate entries, or simply take too long. Time that should be spent running a business is lost to tedious financial admin.

The Solution

Reconex automates the hard parts.

Reconex is a simple, focused tool that takes your raw bank statement, categorises every transaction automatically, and reconciles your closing balance — giving you a clean financial overview in minutes, not hours.
